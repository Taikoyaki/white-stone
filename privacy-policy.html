Privacy Policy for White Stone

Last updated: February 13, 2026

Overview

White Stone ("the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This privacy policy explains how we handle information in our game.

Information We Collect

White Stone stores the following information locally on your device only:

  • Game Progress: Achievement unlocks, stone draw counts, and playtime statistics
  • Settings: Audio volume, graphics quality, and other user preferences
  • Scores: Current game score

How We Use Your Information

All game data is stored locally on your device using encrypted storage. This data is used solely to:

  • Track your achievements and progress
  • Remember your game settings
  • Provide gameplay functionality

Data Storage and Security

  • All data is stored locally on your device only
  • Save files are encrypted using device-unique encryption keys
  • No data is transmitted to external servers
  • No personal information is collected or stored
  • No analytics or tracking services are used

Third-Party Services

White Stone may display advertisements through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect certain information as described in their privacy policy:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • AdMob Data Usage

Children's Privacy

White Stone does not knowingly collect any personal information from children. The game stores only gameplay data locally on the device and does not transmit any information to external servers.

Data Deletion

You can delete all game data at any time by:

  • Uninstalling the app from your device
  • Clearing app data through Android settings (Apps > White Stone > Storage > Clear Data)

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this policy.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at:

Email: support@taikoyaki.com

Your Rights

Since all data is stored locally on your device and no information is transmitted to us, you have complete control over your data. You can delete it at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing its data.
